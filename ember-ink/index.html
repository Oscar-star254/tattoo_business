Questions

How much is the deposit?

25% of your estimate, minimum $30, charged in US dollars and deducted from your final price. Consultations are free.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, with 48 hours' notice email us and we will move your deposit. Later changes forfeit it.

Can you draw my own idea?

Yes. Describe it in the booking form and add the custom drawing option. You approve the sketch first.

How should I care for a new tattoo?

Keep it clean and lightly moisturised, avoid soaking, sun and scratching for two weeks, and follow the care sheet we give you.
